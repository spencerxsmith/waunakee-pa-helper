## Applause

Based on [“Sound Effects - Applause after a concert.ogg”](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sound_Effects_-_Applause_after_a_concert.ogg) by Amada44, dedicated under [Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Edited by trimming, downmixing to mono, normalizing loudness, fading, and converting to MP3. No endorsement is implied.

## Big Cheer

Based on [“Clapping hurray.ogg”](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Clapping_hurray.ogg) by starlite, released into the public domain by its creator. Edited by trimming, downmixing to mono, normalizing loudness, fading, and converting to MP3. No endorsement is implied.
